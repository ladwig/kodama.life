IMPORTANT

There won't be any tickets at the door. Please do not come if you don't have one already — it's not worth the trip.

Getting there and back

Public transport to BER Airport, then follow the ‘Fahrradweg’ signs — a few minutes by bike, or about 35 minutes on foot. By taxi or Uber, get dropped at the marked pick-up point (the roundabout) and walk the last 10 minutes.

IMPORTANT

Driving a car on the bike lane is prohibited — use the marked drop-off point only. Please avoid coming by car; if you really have to, use the marked path and parking area and no other route.

Here are the full routes and maps for each way of getting here. The path is uneven in places, so it's not step-free throughout; get in touch beforehand if you need support.

On the way in and out

We're close to the airport and to people who live nearby. Please don't leave any trash, keep the noise down, and look out for each other. Please do not enter or approach any airport facilities, such as the fence surrounding the runway.

If you arrive or leave after dark there will be limited light, so a flashlight or phone torch will be your friend.

Times

Doors open at 12:00 midday. We expect to close around 11:00 on Sunday morning.

Pack for your adventure

sidequest is loooooooooong and you will need to be prepared. It can get quite chilly after dark and hot during the day. Bring warm layers (a raincoat might be a good idea too), a water bottle, a blanket, sunscreen, enough snacks — whatever is going to carry those legs wayyyy past sunrise.

Bar and food

We've got a well-stocked bar serving up ice cold drinks all day long. Food and snacks are available too. Bring cash!

Membership & entry

sidequest is a private event. No membership (aka ticket) bought in advance, no entry. A ticket doesn't guarantee entry; we reserve the right to refuse at the door.

Sustainability

We intend to leave the site exactly as we found it.

The ground is dry and fire is a real risk, so cigarettes are to be disposed of correctly (we have portable ashtrays available).

Trash goes in the bins (pick up a bit extra whilst you're at it), and Pfand goes back to the bar or in the crates situated around the site.

Zero tolerance

Discrimination, harassment, hate speech, or abusive behaviour based on race, ethnicity, nationality, religion, gender identity or expression, sexual orientation, disability, age, or any other protected characteristic will not be tolerated. We are committed to creating a welcoming, inclusive and accessible space for everyone.

Awareness

Our awareness team will be roaming around the site wearing glowing wristbands and vests. There will also be a dedicated awareness chill-out space if you need a break. If you feel unwell, unsafe or just off at any point, find one of our awareness team members or any member of staff — they're most easily found at the gate or bar. We have an incredibly skilled crew that are always about to support.

Consumption

No open consumption on site. Beyond that, be responsible — with yourself and with everyone around you.
