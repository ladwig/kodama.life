A Sidequest for sidequest

Quests rarely go as planned.
And sidequests are almost entirely unplanned.
For sidequest, unfortunately this seems to have held true.

We promised a gathering amongst the trees, to join the monsters on their journey, to dance, to share, to conspire. The winds of fortune were blowing elsewhere and ultimately the clipboard brigade decided otherwise.

We're pulling up the stakes, rearranging the pieces and heading for a new patch. Not quite as planned but hoping to bring the same sentiment to our new location.

Before we packed it all up, here is what we built out there — a small window into the gathering amongst the trees.
